# Investment Philosophy / 投资哲学

> 代码会被复制，方法论和长期迭代过程更难复制。

This document is the project's moat. It is intentionally separated from the code because **the edge is not in the algorithms — it is in the questions asked and the process repeated.**

---

## Three Beliefs / 三个信念

```
Research  >  Prediction      研究 > 预测
Systems   >  Opinions        系统 > 观点
Long-term >  Short-term      长期 > 短期
```

1. **研究 > 预测.** A repeatable research process beats a one-off "prediction". The market cannot be reliably predicted; it *can* be systematically understood.
2. **系统 > 观点.** A system with explicit, auditable rules beats a charismatic opinion. When the system is wrong, the system is fixed — not the narrative.
3. **长期 > 短期.** Daily noise is traded away; structural understanding compounds.

---

## The Daily Discipline / 每日三步走

The entire system is built to serve one three-step loop:

```
判环境  →  选方向  →  定标的
(Judge the environment)  (Pick the direction)  (Identify the candidate)
```

- **判环境 (Judge the environment):** Is there money in the market at all? Risk-on or risk-off? This is the macro + sentiment gate. If the environment is hostile, the answer is often "do nothing".
- **选方向 (Pick the direction):** Which 1–3 sectors have sustained capital inflow + rising turnover + genuine earnings effect? This is where *板块 > 龙头 > 资金 > 图形* lives.
- **定标的 (Identify the candidate):** Within the chosen sectors, which leaders satisfy the hard filters (capital inflow, trend structure, liquidity)? The system *circles* candidates; the human confirms the chart.

---

## The Daily Three Questions / 每天三问

Every trading day the system answers:

> **今天有没有钱？钱去哪？我怎么办？**
> *(Is there money? Where is it going? What should I do?)*

- **有没有钱** — aggregate capital condition (flow + turnover). If no, the rest is moot.
- **钱去哪** — the migration chain: which sectors are being funded, which are being abandoned.
- **我怎么办** — the actionable output: a circled theme, a candidate list, a position budget, and the invalidation conditions.

---

## Sector > Leader > Capital > Chart / 板块 > 龙头 > 资金 > 图形

This ordering is a hard methodology rule, not a suggestion:

1. **板块 (Sector) first.** Never pick a stock before confirming its sector is in a funded, rising regime. A great company in an unfunded sector is a trap.
2. **龙头 (Leader) second.** Within the sector, prefer the confirmed leader — the name the capital and narrative converge on.
3. **资金 (Capital) third.** Inflows and turnover are the evidence; price alone is not.
4. **图形 (Chart) last.** The chart is the *human's* final confirmation of timing. The AI supplies the data and the zone; the human pulls the trigger.

> **The human is never removed from the loop.** The system "circles the main theme → lists candidates → attaches data". The buy/sell decision is always made by a person reading the chart.

---

## Why "Research", not "Prediction" / 为什么是"研究"而非"预测"

A prediction tool optimizes for being *right about price*. A research operating system optimizes for *understanding the environment well enough to act with discipline*. The latter is honest about uncertainty, exposes its reasoning, and improves by replaying its own past calls — which is exactly what the Learning Center does.

This is also why the project is named an **Operating System**, not a "framework" or a "bot": it is personal research infrastructure, not a product that replaces judgment.
